# Dev 50 figure-quality diagnosis

## Scope and method

- Read-only `figure_quality_eval.py --set dev` replay over the frozen 50 keys; no vault writes. Replay output: 50 papers, 254 matched figure records; harness reports 50 passed / 0 failed (structural gate only, not a visual-quality pass).
- Retained one `baseline.json` per key plus `result.json`; compact diagnostics include per-paper evidence and per-figure review labels. Large replay image sets remain in `.work/dev50` and are not included.
- Layer buckets overlap and are screening signals, not defect adjudications. `caption_candidate` includes candidate rows whether or not another association exists. Multi-page-candidate pages are pages containing unmatched captions/assets, not proof of a cross-page claim.

## Recurring patterns and Q11 recommendation

1. **Q11 candidate — current asset-file delivery gap.** 174 non-reserved matched figure IDs across 34/50 papers lack `assets/figures/<id>.jpg`, while render markdown and fulltext embed targets are present. This is the broadest directly verifiable on-disk issue; confirm that the asset file is a required deliverable before changing generation behavior.
2. **Q11 candidate — duplicate figure association.** Repeated figure IDs occur in six papers; one same-page duplicate asset owner occurs in R8PCX2YL, and the sampled 6A8A9TWD duplicate-ID/claim was visually confirmed as a wrong Figure 1 asset also assigned to Figure 2. A narrow duplicate-ID/same-page-owner audit is provable; prevalence of actual visual misassociation needs review.

**HOLD:** caption-recall flags (17 unmatched legends, 34 candidate rows), unmatched assets (54), panel/crop proxies, large groups, and multi-page candidate spread remain unverified heuristics; sample review did not confirm caption, crop-boundary, panel-split/merge, or cross-page defects. No missing render markdown, dangling embed, text decrease, or pending marker was found. Table claim changes were seen once; hold absent independent visual confirmation.
